SeaLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SeaLog, an Android application provided under the developer name SeaLog Studio, handles information. For questions, contact sealogstudio@gmail.com.

1. Information you enter

SeaLog allows you to create vessel profiles, technical records, descriptions, checklists and tags, and attach photographs, documents, video and audio. This content is stored locally on your device. SeaLog does not operate a cloud library that automatically uploads these records.

2. Local storage and security

SeaLog uses encrypted local database storage. You control the information you enter. Password-protected backup export and restore features are available; exported backups are saved to a location you select. You are responsible for protecting backup files and passwords.

3. Device permissions

Depending on the feature you choose, SeaLog may request microphone access for audio recording, notification permission, or device biometric authentication. The application may use Android foreground processing for media-related tasks. Permissions can be managed in Android settings.

4. Exporting, opening and sharing files

When you choose to export, open, or share an attachment or backup, the application may create temporary readable files for the requested operation. Information you choose to send to another app or service is subject to that recipient's privacy practices. SeaLog does not automatically send your vessel records to SeaLog Studio.

5. Support communications

If you choose to contact support by email, your email provider sends the message and any details you decide to include to SeaLog Studio. We use those details to respond to your inquiry and troubleshoot issues. Avoid including confidential vessel information unless necessary and authorized.

6. Analytics, advertising and payments

The current free test build does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, advertising SDKs, or in-app purchases. This policy will be updated if these practices change.

7. Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the application's data, subject to any in-app trash retention and backups you have created. Files exported to other locations are not deleted when you delete local SeaLog records. Support emails are retained as needed to handle inquiries and related correspondence; you may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

8. Your choices and requests

You can edit and delete records within SeaLog, manage device permissions in Android settings, and request information about or deletion of support communications by emailing sealogstudio@gmail.com. Locally stored records are controlled on your device; SeaLog Studio cannot remotely access or delete them.

9. Children

SeaLog is intended as a professional maritime information tool and is not designed for children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The effective date at the top will be updated accordingly.
